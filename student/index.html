    Help and support

    Why can't I request an item?

    The item may be out of stock, you may already have a pending or approved request for it, the dates may be in the past or longer than 14 days, or the signature does not exactly match your registered full name.

    I forgot my password

    Use "Forgot password?" on the login page. PEMU will give you a temporary password at the office.

    Where do I get and return the item?

    After PEMU approves your request, claim the item at the PEMU office and bring it back there on or before the return date.

    The page shows old data or an offline message

    Check your internet connection. The page refreshes by itself every few seconds once you are back online.

    Need a quick answer?

    Ask the assistant (the glowing orb at the bottom left), for example "What can I borrow?". Otherwise visit the PEMU office.
